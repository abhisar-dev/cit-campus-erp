Knowledge Base & Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding timetables, venue booking, and exam seating rules

1. How does the OR-Tools CP-SAT engine eliminate timetable clashes? ▼
The CIT scheduling engine is powered by Google OR-Tools CP-SAT (Constraint Programming with Satisfiability). Every student cohort, professor, and classroom slot is modeled mathematically with hard non-overlap constraints (H1: No professor in two rooms at once; H2: No cohort in two lectures at once; H3: Room capacity strictly respected). The schedule guarantees zero hard mathematical clashes.
2. What is Rule H5 regarding Midsem Examination Seating? ▼
Under CIT Examination Regulation H5, during midsemester and endsemester examinations, all testing halls operate under a mandatory 50% capacity distancing policy. For example, a 60-seat lecture hall admits a maximum of 30 test candidates. This ensures academic integrity and fair invigilation.
3. Why was my Spectra club booking rejected or rescheduled (Rule H6)? ▼
Under Institutional Rule H6, curricular examinations and scheduled academic lectures maintain precedence over extracurricular bookings. If an examination block overlaps with a provisional student club reservation, the system automatically redirects the club booking to alternative periods or requests administrative clearance from the Dean.
4. How does the AI Venue Booking Assistant work with Hinglish inputs? ▼
Our AI Venue Booking Assistant features an NLP regex parser tuned for Indian campus vernacular. It recognizes natural queries in English, Hindi, and Hinglish such as "Auditorium AUD-01 chahiye Friday P6 se P8 tak for drama rehearsal", extracts venue parameters, and performs an instant constraint verification against current bookings.
5. What protocol is followed if a faculty member is on medical leave? ▼
When a faculty absence is cleared by the Dean's office (such as Faculty F07 on approved leave), the system runs an emergency minimal-move reassignment algorithm. Substitute professors are assigned while preserving 98%+ of student cohort stability to prevent campus-wide disruption.
6. What should I do if my browser displays an outdated timetable? ▼
CIT ERP utilizes service caching for fast mobile access. If you suspect an updated timetable hasn't rendered, press Ctrl + Shift + R (or Cmd + Shift + R on macOS) to hard refresh your cache, or log out and log back in to renew your authentication token.
