01

Institutional Overview & Governance Scope

The Chilika Institute of Technology (CIT) Campus ERP serves as the central operational backbone for academic timetabling, venue management during the annual Spectra Tech-Cultural Fest, midsemester examination seating allocations, and real-time campus disruption mitigation.

This Privacy Policy applies to all students, faculty members, visiting professors, departmental coordinators, administrative staff, and student club representatives accessing the system via:

  • Student & Faculty Portal: Weekly cohort timetable, room schedule lookup, and midsem exam allocations.
  • Spectra Venue Booking Desk: Automated club request processing and priority ordering.
  • AI Natural Language Booking Assistant: English and Hinglish conversational venue reservation query engine.
  • Admin Command Terminal: Dean's Office schedule generation, disruption repair (CR-102 rerouting), and validator audit.
Institutional Mandate: All academic scheduling algorithms adhere strictly to the guidelines certified by the official CIT academic validator (validator.py), ensuring complete fairness, transparency, and elimination of arbitrary scheduling conflicts.
02

CIT Campus ERP strictly adheres to the principle of Data Minimization. We only collect and compute records directly indispensable to campus operations:

Category Data Elements Operational Purpose

Deterministic priority scoring (H6 constraint), avoiding priority inversions and assigning suitable venues (e.g. Auditorium AUD-01).
Security & Session State Browser local storage session token (cit_session), active clearance level, IP timestamp log for administrative actions. Authenticating access between Dean Admin Terminal and student-facing timetable views.
03

Constraint-Driven Optimization & Algorithmic Ethics

Unlike traditional administrative workflows where room or schedule assignments could be subject to subjective bias or opaque human discretion, CIT Campus ERP utilizes a mathematical Constraint Satisfaction Solver (Google OR-Tools CP-SAT).

The solver treats all cohorts and student groups with mathematically provable neutrality:

  • Zero Preference Bias: No cohort or faculty is prioritized unless explicitly codified in official Senate constraints (e.g. Faculty medical leaves or hard room capacity limits).
  • Explainability by Design: Every booking rejection or alternative offering includes an unalterable algorithmic decision rationale (e.g. "Higher priority Fest Core requested Auditorium AUD-01; alternative offered in Seminar Hall SEM-01").
  • Disruption Stability (Minimal Move): When emergency maintenance occurs (such as the ceiling leak disruption in CR-102), the system minimizes perturbation by relocating room assignments without modifying students' established class periods.
Audit Certified: The baseline timetable submitted achieves 0 Hard Violations across all 14 hard constraints (H1 through H14) and 0 Priority Inversions, verifying ethical algorithmic fairness.
04

Midsem Examination Logistics & Seating Privacy (H5 Rule)

During midsemester examination cycles, student test integrity is protected using automated spatial distancing rules rather than invasive surveillance software:

  • 50% Capacity Seating Limit (Constraint H5): Each examination hall is allocated to at most half of its certified seating capacity (e.g., a 60-seat classroom admits a maximum of 30 test candidates).
  • Zero Non-Academic Biometric Profiling: The ERP does not utilize facial recognition, keystroke logging, or video proctoring feeds. Seating assignments are strictly spatial and room-based.
  • Cohort Isolation: Overlapping student groups are never assigned multiple simultaneous exams, guaranteeing a fair resting interval between 3-hour test blocks (P2–P4 and P6–P8).
05

AI Venue Booking Assistant & Natural Language (NLP) Processing

The platform integrates a specialized conversational AI assistant supporting both formal English and campus Hinglish queries (e.g. "Auditorium AUD-01 chahiye Friday P6 se P8 tak for drama club rehearsal").

Your Prompt Privacy Guarantee:
  • Conversational queries are parsed ephemerally into structured booking parameters (venue, day, period, attendance, equipment needs).
  • No Model Retraining: User conversation history is never fed into public training corpuses or external LLM databases.
  • No Telemetry Sharing: Inquiries are evaluated directly against CIT's local database and timetable schedule constraints.
06

Role-Based Access Control & Privilege Tiers

Data accessibility is demarcated into 4 strict privilege levels to prevent unauthorized elevation of privilege:

Role Tier Authorized Scope Restrictions
Student View cohort timetable, filter courses, view allocated midsem exam rooms, check Spectra booking status. Read-only for schedules; cannot alter room assignments or view faculty personal leaves.
Faculty Member View personal weekly teaching schedule, track daily teaching hours against 4-hour caps, request rescheduling. Cannot edit other faculty schedules or override institute exams.
Club Representative Submit Spectra venue booking requests, check AI availability, view approval reasons or alternatives. Subject to precedence validation; cannot approve their own requests.
Dean / Central Admin Execute CP-SAT solver, approve emergency disruptions (e.g. CR-102 repair), reset demo states, export official artifacts. All actions logged; clearances restricted to verified administrative credentials.
07

Client-Side Storage & Session Management

CIT Campus ERP does not employ commercial tracking cookies, advertising beacons, or third-party behavioral pixels.

We utilize browser localStorage exclusively for operational persistence:

  • cit_session: Stores user identity, verified email, role tag, and cohort preference for session continuity.
  • Clearing your browser cache or clicking "Sign Out" instantly invalidates and deletes the stored session key.
08

Absolute Prohibition on Commercial Data Monetization

Chilika Institute of Technology does not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize student or faculty data under any circumstances.

Academic timetables, room schedules, and event booking logs remain the exclusive sovereign property of the institute. Information is never shared with third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertiser networks.

09

Data Retention & Administrator Demo Resets

Active timetables and examination distributions are retained for the duration of the academic semester. Following semester conclusion, historical schedules are archived into the institute's permanent academic registry.

Evaluator & Hackathon Demo Reset Notice: For the purposes of technical judging and verification, the system provides a Dean-level "↺ Reset Baseline State" endpoint. This resets the in-memory dataset, cancels temporary room reroutes, and restores the certified 0-clash baseline for fresh validation runs.
10

Rights of Students, Faculty & Event Leads

Under institutional policy and applicable data protection regulations, users hold the following rights:

  • Right to Audit: Any student or faculty member may inspect their assigned timetable and view the mathematical justification for room allocations.
  • Right to Rectification: In the event of an erroneous cohort assignment or student group mismatch, members may request an administrative update through their Department Academic Coordinator.
  • Right of Withdrawal: Club representatives may cancel pending venue booking requests at any time prior to event commencement without penalty.
