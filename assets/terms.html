01

Acceptance of Institutional Terms & Scope

By accessing, browsing, or utilizing the Chilika Institute of Technology (CIT) Campus ERP (including the Weekly Academic Timetable, Spectra Fest Venue Booking desk, AI Booking Assistant, and Midsem Examination Portal), you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions, the CIT Student Handbook, and official Academic Senate resolutions.

These terms apply to all undergraduate (B.Tech Year 1 cohorts Y1-A, Y1-B, Y1-C, Y1-D), departmental year 2–3 cohorts (CSE-2, ECE-2, EE-2, ME-2, CE-2), and recognized student campus organizations.

Official Regulation: Access to the portal is a privilege granted solely for legitimate academic scheduling, examination logistics, and authorized co-curricular venue management.
02

Student Accounts, Credentials & Clearance Integrity

Every student registered on the portal must strictly adhere to the following identity policies:

  • Official Institute Email: Student accounts must be registered using an authorized @cit.ac.in email address. External disposable emails are automatically rejected.
  • Non-Transferability: Account session tokens (cit_session) and passwords must remain confidential. Sharing credentials to manipulate cohort attendance or book venues on behalf of unauthorized external third parties is strictly prohibited.
  • No Role Impersonation: Students are strictly prohibited from attempting to bypass role barriers, forge administrative session tokens, or access the Dean's Admin Command Terminal without authorization.
03

Academic Timetable Adherence & Cohort Protocol

Weekly academic timetables generated by the CIT optimization engine are certified to contain 0 Hard Clashes across student groups, faculty schedules, and certified lecture room capacities:

  • Standard Period Schedule: Academic instruction occurs Monday through Friday across Periods P1 to P9 (09:00 to 17:50).
  • Mandatory Lunch Recess (Period P5): Period P5 (13:00 to 13:50) is permanently reserved as the Institute Lunch Recess (Constraint H14). No lectures, laboratory sessions, or student meetings may be scheduled during this interval.
  • Cohort Integrity: Students must attend sessions allotted specifically to their assigned cohort (e.g. Y1-A, CSE-2). Unauthorized swapping between lecture cohorts without Department Academic Coordinator approval is invalid.
Guarantee of Zero Clashes: Students will never be assigned two concurrent required academic classes (Constraint H3). If you detect an anomaly, report it immediately to your Department Academic Secretariat.
04

Midsemester Examination Logistics & Anti-Cheating Protocol (H5)

Midsemester examinations are scheduled in certified 3-period blocks (P2–P4 and P6–P8) subject to strict physical distancing and seating regulations:

Rule Code Institutional Constraint Student Obligation
H5 Seating Rule Hall Capacity Halved (e.g. 60-seat hall accommodates max 30 candidates). Sit strictly at designated desk numbers. Occupying vacant alternating desks is prohibited.
Identity Proof Official CIT Student ID Verification by Invigilators. Carry your physical biometric student card to every examination block.
H8 Group Isolation Zero student group overlaps across simultaneous examinations. Arrive 15 minutes before the period window commences (09:45 for P2; 13:45 for P6).
Zero-Tolerance Malpractice: Any attempt to compromise seating allocations, communicate across desks, or bring unapproved electronic devices into examination venues results in immediate paper cancellation and referral to the Institute Academic Disciplinary Committee.
05

Spectra Tech-Cultural Fest Venue Allocation Terms

During Spectra Tech-Fest Week 7, student clubs and campus societies may reserve institute venues (Auditorium AUD-01, Seminar Halls SEM-01/SEM-02, Open Air Theatre OAT, Classrooms) through the portal:

  • Deterministic Priority Precedence (Constraint H6): Allocation follows strict institutional hierarchy: Fest Core Events > Technical Societies > Cultural & Literary Clubs > Informal Gatherings. Lower-priority clubs cannot displace higher-priority events.
  • Alternative Venue Policy: If a preferred venue is contested by higher priority, the engine may propose a certified alternative venue (e.g. Seminar Hall SEM-01 instead of AUD-01). Club leads have 24 hours to confirm or withdraw.
  • Property & Asset Liability: The student club representative submitting the booking is personally accountable for the preservation of audio/visual equipment, projectors, and venue cleanliness.
06

AI Booking Assistant & NLP Fair Usage Policy

The integrated Natural Language Booking Assistant accepts plain English and Hinglish queries to accelerate room availability checks. To preserve server responsiveness for all campus members, the following rules apply:

  • Academic Inquiries Only: Use the assistant solely for checking room schedules, lecture slots, and legitimate venue availability inquiries.
  • No Automated Scraping or Bot Flooding: Scripted automated pinging, denial-of-service attempts, or prompt injection payloads are prohibited and logged with client IP timestamps.
  • Ephemeral Computation: Queries are parsed in memory and are not used for commercial model training or private surveillance.
07

Campus Disruptions & Minimal-Move Rescheduling

In situations of unforeseen facility disruptions (e.g., pipe leakage in Lecture Hall CR-102 or sudden faculty medical leave), the Dean's Office activates the Minimal-Move Repair Engine:

  • Room Relocation Notification: Disrupted sessions are relocated to equivalent available rooms (e.g. CR-101) without shifting class timing wherever feasible.
  • Student Obligation: Students must inspect the Weekly Timetable dashboard for real-time room reassignment tags (marked with red disruption notices) before proceeding to class.

09

Violations & Disciplinary Procedures

Failure to adhere to these terms may result in one or more of the following sanctions determined by the Dean of Student Welfare:

  • Tier 1: Temporary revocation of student club venue booking privileges for up to one academic semester.
  • Tier 2: Immediate cancellation of conflicting venue bookings and forfeiture of security clearances.
  • Tier 3: Formal disciplinary referral under the CIT Student Academic Integrity Charter with notation on official transcripts.
