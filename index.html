Institutional Privacy Policy

Chilika Institute of Technology · Academic Data Privacy Charter

Zero Commercial Monetization Commitment:

CIT operates this autonomous scheduling platform solely for academic timetables, exam room seatings, and verified venue allocations. Your personal and academic records are strictly safeguarded and never tracked or sold.

1. Conflict-Free Scheduling (Rules H1–H14)
All timetable and venue allocations follow deterministic mathematical algorithms (OR-Tools CP-SAT) with zero hard clashes and complete cohort equity.
2. Anti-Cheating & Exam Privacy (Rule H5)
Midsemester seating guarantees 50% capacity halving to ensure exam integrity via physical spacing, without invasive biometrics or webcam tracking.
3. AI Booking Assistant & Query Confidentiality
Conversational Hinglish & English queries are parsed ephemerally on-premise. No prompts or user details are logged into external public LLMs.
4. Local Session Authentication
Authentication is isolated locally (cit_session) without tracking cookies. Signing out instantly flushes stored keys.
